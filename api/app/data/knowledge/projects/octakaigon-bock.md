# Octakaigon Bock — Quantum Design & Research Internship

**Photonic QPU circuit optimization and Quantum OS contributions. Internship role.**

## What I work on

Two threads:

1. **Photonic QPU circuit optimization.** Python-based simulation and optimization pipelines for circuits running on 100-qubit photonic quantum processors. Got a 20% efficiency gain on the optimization pass during my contribution window.
2. **Quantum OS.** Contributed to the work on scheduling, resource management, and hardware abstraction. The "OS" framing is the right one — quantum hardware is heterogeneous and the dev experience needs an abstraction layer that lets researchers write circuits without manually managing qubit allocation, calibration cycles, and cooldown windows.

## Stack

Python, numpy, plus the in-house simulation libraries Octakaigon develops internally. I won't go into the proprietary specifics.

## Why I do this alongside SOYL

Quantum is a different kind of problem than agent systems and the context-switch is actively useful. The SOYL work is about engineering at scale and dealing with messy real-world data; the quantum work is about precise physical models and squeezing the last percent out of well-defined optimization problems. Doing both stops me from getting stale on either.
